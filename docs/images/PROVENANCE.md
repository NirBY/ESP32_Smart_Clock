  six faithful white rounded enclosures with two feet and external sensor cage.
  Demonstrate clock, countdown, temperature/humidity, custom messages, daily
  alarm, music/speaker. English headings, Free STL, creator credit, and
  “HA MCP SCRIPTS INCLUDED • Setup required”. Only short USB plugs, no cables.
  Label display modes illustrative; no certification or unsupported print claims.

  three rows, retaining all six features, wording, hardware proportions and
  the no-cable constraint.
- `promo-hero.png`: single product hero, blue HELLO! display, “YOUR TEXT.
  YOUR CLOCK.” and “Send messages from Home Assistant”; same visual style.
  Remove the cable while retaining the short plug.

  distraction while preserving the pictured hardware and wiring. These images
  are illustrative; original photos are provided for comparison.

## Animated GitHub showcase

`showcase-storyboard.png` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool from
`promo-4x3.png`: eight independent English feature slides in the established
navy/cyan style, preserving the white two-foot clock and short USB plug without
a cable. Topics: time, countdown, temperature/humidity, custom messages, daily
alarm schedules, audio, Home Assistant/MCP (setup required), and printable enclosure.
Every slide is labeled illustrative. The exploded enclosure illustration is
promotional, not a CAD reference; use the STL renders and guide for assembly.
`tools/build_showcase.py` extracts the supplied panels and encodes an eight-frame,
four-seconds-per-frame looping GIF; it does not generate or alter hardware details.
