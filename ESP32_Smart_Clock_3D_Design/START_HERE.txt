ESP32 Smart Clock enclosure - final design revision 18
Creator: NirBY | License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

Print one each: stl/body.stl, stl/lid.stl, stl/sensor_cage.stl.
Units: mm. Scale: 100%. Keep supplied print orientations.

BODY: white PLA, 0.20 mm first and later layers. At least three solid
bottom layers form the 0.6 mm LED diffuser. Opaque black PLA blocks light.

LID: fixed 0.15 mm first AND later layers. Pause AFTER completing Z 4.05 mm
(27 layers), BEFORE Z 4.20 mm. Install two M3 brass inserts, length 2.9 mm,
in the USB-side posts. Their tops must sit below the paused surface.
The 0.6 mm rear closures are integral to the lid, not separate parts.

Read PRINT_GUIDE.pdf for the exact insert procedure, fasteners and assembly.
The complete latest assembly and pause workflow are not physically validated.

Optional fit samples and editable source are in the Creality Upload and
GitHub Source packages, or the project repository:
https://github.com/NirBY/ESP32_Smart_Clock
